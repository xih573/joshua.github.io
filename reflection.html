Design Reflection Notes
01

Typography

Although I’ve tried different fonts, for actual websites, sans-serif fonts are more efficient, concise, and easy to read, while also fitting various styles.

02

Composition

I prefer a simple style in layout. When there isn't too much content, I tend to design the whole site on a single page, allowing users to browse naturally through scrolling.

03

Evolution

From "Walk" to the "Visual Only" site, as I learned more, my design freedom increased. At first, I had cool ideas I couldn't code, making things a bit boring, but now I can realize them—though I've always tried to keep it clean.

04

Learnings

So far, besides basic HTML and CSS, I've also learned about SVG, layout techniques, and more along the way, and for future, I hope to see more css style and even Javascript, I can't wait to apply these skills in my futre work
