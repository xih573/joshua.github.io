I step into the store.

Rows of full bookshelves. I see hundreds of used manga, games, and CDs.

Some thoughts in my head

I came here today with no specific shopping list. I decided to browse slowly through every aisle, with zero pressure to buy anything—just enjoying the quiet time passing by.

My route

  1. I think about the vintage games and anime figures I used to admire.
  2. Walk straight past the new arrivals display and into the quiet book aisles.
  3. Turn the corner into the manga and figure display area.
  4. Keep going until you reach the shelves of old DVDs and trading cards.
  5. Wander aimlessly upstairs just to enjoy the peaceful atmosphere.

Things I noticed

  • A shopper quietly scanning through character figures inside bright showcase cases.
  • Rows of rare trading cards displayed behind glass.
  • Endless stacks of neatly organized second-hand books and Gundam plastic model boxes.

The model kits & card section

The shelf of Gunpla boxes looked overwhelming at first. Looking closer, I noticed detailed designs, nostalgic series, and classic artwork.

The quiet aisles

Running my fingers along the book spines without picking one up to buy. Just being here feels therapeutic.

Words from a sign

After the walk

I ended up buying nothing at all today. But spending an hour just wandering around, revisiting old memories, and wasting some time was surprisingly comforting and wonderful.

Notes written from memory after returning home with empty hands and a peaceful mind.
