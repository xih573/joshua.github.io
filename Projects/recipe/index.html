HOW TO
TIME TRAVEL

A simple guide to
traveling into the future.

By Joshua

What You Need

1. A bed

2. A pillow

3. A blanket

4. An alarm clock

Instructions

Step 1

Set an alarm for around 6:00 or 7:00 in the morning.
(The time is only a recommendation. Set the alarm based on your own schedule.)

Step 2

When the alarm rings, turn it off.

Step 3

Tell yourself, "I will sleep for five more minutes."

Step 4

Close your eyes and go back to sleep.

Step 5

When you wake up again, check the time. You may discover that one or two hours have passed.
