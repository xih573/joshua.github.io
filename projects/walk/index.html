Joshua's Diary

An afternoon
at BOOKOFF.

A walk, remembered. BOOKOFF / NYC

Like any other day, I walked into the store with no particular purpose. Hundreds of used manga, games, figures, and CDs were lined up just as usual. I wandered aimlessly through the aisles, checking which items had sold out, what was newly stocked, and spotting the IPs I recognized. Just enjoying the quiet time slipping away.

I thought about the games and anime figures I used to want so badly.

Walking into the quiet book aisles, turning toward the manga and figure displays, keeping going past the DVDs and games, and stopping whenever something caught my eye.

Passing by that shelf of Gunpla model kits, I just couldn't move my feet anymore. I stood there quietly, mentally reciting their names and seeing how many I still recognized.

Just being here
feels therapeutic.

Quiet aisles.

Looking at the endless shelves of books, letting my fingers slide across the spines, and admiring the artists' beautiful strokes. Even without buying anything, my eyes were already fully satisfied.

The view on the wall.

A yellow board covered with flyers and store notices. Big headlines, small print, and different colors, all sharing the same space. A few small details before leaving.

After a little inner struggle, I ended up buying nothing today. But spending an hour just wandering around, revisiting old memories, and wasting some time was surprisingly comforting and wonderful.

Empty hands, peaceful mind.

— Joshua
