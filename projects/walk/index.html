A walk, remembered. BOOKOFF / NYC

01 / Stepping inside

mentally i'm here.

I step into the store. Rows of full bookshelves. I see hundreds of used manga, games, and CDs.

I came here today with no specific shopping list. I decided to browse slowly through every aisle, with zero pressure to buy anything—just enjoying the quiet time passing by.

02 / Wandering

No particular
direction.

I think about the vintage games and anime figures I used to admire.

  1. Walk into the quiet book aisles.
  2. Turn toward the manga and figure displays.
  3. Keep going past the DVDs and games.
  4. Stop whenever something catches my eye.

No rush. No checklist.

03 / Small discoveries

Little things I stopped for.

The shelf of Gunpla boxes looked overwhelming at first. Looking closer, I noticed detailed designs, nostalgic series, and classic artwork.

A thought between the aisles

Just being here
feels therapeutic.

04 / Slowing down

The quiet aisles.

Running my fingers along the book spines without picking one up to buy.

For a little while, I was happy to keep looking.

05 / On the wall

One more thing
to look at.

A yellow board covered with flyers and store notices. Big headlines, small print, and different colors, all sharing the same space.

Even the walls had something to say.

06 / Heading home

Leaving with nothing.

I ended up buying nothing at all today. But spending an hour just wandering around, revisiting old memories, and wasting some time was surprisingly comforting and wonderful.

Empty hands, peaceful mind.

— Joshua

Notes written from memory after returning home.
