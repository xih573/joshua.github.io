I step into the store.

Rows of full bookshelves. Hundreds of used manga, games, and collectibles.

Explore the walk ↓

Some thoughts in my head

I came here today with no specific shopping list. I decided to browse slowly through every aisle, with zero pressure to buy anything—just enjoying the quiet time passing by.

Looking at the boxes, I kept recognizing characters and remembering things I used to like.

My route

  1. Pause at the collectibles and think about the games and anime figures I used to admire.
  2. Walk into the book aisles.
  3. Slow down to look at the manga and model kits.
  4. Keep going toward the shelves of DVDs and games.
  5. Wander without deciding what to buy.

Things I noticed

  • Colorful figure boxes packed tightly along the shelves.
  • Game controllers arranged behind glass.
  • Long rows of book spines, interrupted by bright signs.

The model kit section

The shelf of Gunpla boxes looked overwhelming at first. Looking closer, I noticed detailed designs, nostalgic series, and classic artwork .

Each box had so much to look at. I spent more time looking at the packaging than thinking about buying anything.

The quiet aisles

Running my fingers along the book spines without picking one up to buy.

Just being here feels therapeutic.

A pause by the games

I stopped at the glass cabinet to look at the controllers. Seeing familiar shapes made me think about the games I used to play.

It was another reason to slow down and stay a little longer.

A wall of information

Away from the shelves, a yellow board was filled with flyers and store notices.

Large headlines, small print, and bright colors competed for my attention. I found myself looking at the arrangement before reading the words.

After the walk

I ended up buying nothing at all today. But spending an hour just wandering around, revisiting old memories, and wasting some time was surprisingly comforting and wonderful.

I came in without a shopping list. I left with empty hands and a peaceful mind.
